# Competitor benchmark (short)

All competitor statements come from the official pages below, read on 2026-11-25. Statements about HelixBoard come from this audit. "Not checked" means I did not test it, so do not treat it as missing.

## Sources
- Microsoft Whiteboard, features list: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/whiteboard/whiteboard-features-and-functionality
- Microsoft Whiteboard, ruler and Enhance Shapes: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/whiteboard/use-ruler-and-enhance-shapes-in-whiteboard
- Microsoft Whiteboard, templates: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/whiteboard/insert-templates-in-whiteboard
- Explain Everything, importing documents and presentations: https://help.explaineverything.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019804873-Import-documents-spreadsheets-and-presentations
- Explain Everything, home page: https://explaineverything.com/
- Google Jamboard shutdown notice: https://support.google.com/jamboard/answer/14084927

## What the official pages say
- Microsoft Whiteboard lists: text, drawing and ink, comments, notes and note grids, ruler and "Enhance Shapes" (hand-drawn shapes turn into clean ones), inserting pictures and documents, locking content, templates (you can make and share your own), annotation while sharing a screen in Teams, "Follow" to guide others through a board, links, online videos, and Loop components.
- Explain Everything says you can import documents, spreadsheets and presentations to annotate or build on, record video tutorials, and collaborate in real time.
- Google Jamboard was shut down; the notice says it is no longer available after 31 December 2024. Classrooms that used it need a replacement.

## Where HelixBoard stands (from the audit)
| Area | Competitors | HelixBoard 3.3.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Ink to clean shapes | Whiteboard "Enhance Shapes" | Has "Ink to shapes", Straighten, Smooth |
| Templates | Whiteboard (own templates can be shared) | Has note and science templates, 64 backgrounds, one searchable gallery now |
| Import PDF / PowerPoint / Word | Explain Everything imports documents and presentations | Has all three, checked against real files in 3.3.0 |
| Screen annotation | Whiteboard in Teams | Has "Annotate screen" |
| Real-time shared board | Both | No. By decision, only a design doc (docs/) |
| Comments, lock content, link/video embeds, Follow | Whiteboard | Not checked |
| Science content (cell atlas, Punnett, molecules, mini-labs, roots dictionary), Arabic/RTL and Hijri dates, smartboard pen speed | Not in the pages above | Yes. This is HelixBoard's real edge for a biology teacher |
| Cost and hardware | Not compared here | Free, Windows app plus phones |

Take-away: the gap is sharing with other people (real-time board, comments, follow). The strength is subject tools and Arabic. A "lock content" tool and a "comments" tool are the cheapest next additions, if they are really missing (check first).
